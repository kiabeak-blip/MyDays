Account Deletion Request

How to delete your account

1
Open the MyDays app on your device.
2
Go to Settings (bottom navigation bar).
3
Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email to request account deletion. Include the email address associated with your MyDays account.

Request via Email

What data is deleted

  • Your account credentials (email and password)
  • Your family group and all member profiles
  • All tasks and calendar data
  • All subtasks and completion history
⚠️ Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Family members linked to your account will also lose access.
